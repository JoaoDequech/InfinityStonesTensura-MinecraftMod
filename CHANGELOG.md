# Changelog

## 1.0.0
- First official release.
- New abilities for every stone, new stone combinations and new Infinity Gauntlet powers.
- Power wheel: second page per stone (scroll over the center) and Shift stats panel.
- Infinity Gauntlet: stones stay inside the gauntlet when it is dropped or stored; new gauntlet page at the top of the power wheel with a stone socket screen.
- Climate Zone: nine new climates.
- Paint the World: palette menu with "back to normal".
- Reworked Sky Fist, Lucid Dream, Harmless Form, Nightmare and Soul World return position.
- Claims: only block changes inside other players' claims are protected.
- Performance improvements, bug fixes and balance adjustments.
