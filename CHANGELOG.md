# Changelog

## 1.0.0
First official release! A cozy update with new abilities, quality-of-life touches and fresh visuals. Have fun! ✨

### Highlights
- New abilities for every stone, new stone combinations and new Infinity Gauntlet powers.
- Power wheel: a second page for each stone (scroll over the center) and a stats panel when holding Shift.
- Abilities use a bit of your magicules when you cast them.

### Infinity Gauntlet
- Your stones now stay safely inside the gauntlet when you drop it, store it in a chest or pick it back up.
- New gauntlet page at the top of the power wheel, with little lights showing which stones are in place.
- New stone screen: click a socket to take a stone out, or click a stone to put it in. "Insert all" and "Remove all" buttons are there too.
- The gauntlet is recognized wherever you carry it: in your hands, in the accessory slot or in your inventory.

### Abilities
- **Sky Fist:** a much bigger and prettier arm now comes down from a magic circle in the sky.
- **Climate Zone:** nine new climates: Blizzard, Ashfall, Cherry Blossom, Supercell, Meteor Shower, Toxic Fog, Starry Night, Heatwave and Rainbow.
- **Paint the World:** a palette menu, with a "Back to normal" option whenever you want your world back.
- **Lucid Dream:** now a fun building tool, with a golden preview, a shape menu, size on the scroll wheel and undo.
- **Harmless Form:** now really turns players and mobs into chickens. 🐔
- **Nightmare:** spookier than ever, with darkness, strange sounds and shadows at the edge of your view.
- **Soul World:** leaving brings you and your friends back to the exact spot where you entered.

### Skill tree
- Fixed a few abilities that couldn't be unlocked because they were overlapping other nodes.

### Claims (Open Parties and Claims)
- Claims protect blocks, so other players' builds stay safe.
- Playing with friends works everywhere: teleports, effects and abilities are no longer blocked by claims.

### Stability
- Bug fixes and small performance improvements.
